# Customer Support Handbook

How shopping on {store_name} works. Exact amounts (shipping prices, cash on delivery limit, return window, points)
come from the store settings and are listed in the store facts the assistant also reads.

## About the Marketplace

{store_name} is a marketplace: many independent sellers sell here, and every seller is reviewed before they can sell.
One order can contain items from several sellers. Each seller packs and ships their own items, so one order can
arrive in several parcels, each with its own shipping method and tracking number.

## Creating an Account

Customers register with their name, email and a password, or sign in with Google when that button is shown.
After registering, a verification email is sent; open the link in it to verify the email address.
An account keeps orders, returns, addresses, wishlist, loyalty points and email preferences in one place.

## Profile and Password

Under Account, then Profile, customers can change their name and phone number and change their password
(the current password is needed). Accounts created with Google can sign in with Google at any time.

## Email Verification

A verification link is emailed after registering. If it did not arrive, check the spam folder; the link can be sent
again from Account, then Profile.

## Addresses

Save several delivery addresses under Account, then Addresses, and choose a default one. At checkout any saved
address can be picked or a new one entered. The address of an order that is already placed cannot be changed by
the customer; if it is wrong, talk to the support team quickly, before the order ships.

## Forgot Password

On the sign-in page choose "Forgot password", enter the account email and open the reset link that is emailed.
The link expires after a while; request a new one if it no longer works. Still locked out? Talk to the support team.

## Browsing and Searching

Search from the top of every page, or browse by category, by brand and by seller (each seller has a store page
with their products, rating and policies). Product pages show the price, variants (size, colour, storage …),
stock, the seller, reviews and questions. Prices can be shown in other currencies and the site language can be
changed with the switcher in the header.

## Out of Stock and Notify Me

When a product or variant is out of stock, the product page offers "Notify me": enter an email and an email is sent
when it is back in stock. Stock is shared across the seller's warehouses and updates in real time.

## Placing an Order

Add products to the cart, then go to checkout. Enter or pick a delivery address, choose a shipping option for each
seller, add a coupon, gift card or loyalty points if you have them, choose a payment method and place the order.
A confirmation email with the order number follows. Order numbers look like ORD-261011-7K2QX.
Items are reserved while an online payment is in progress.

## Payment Methods

Available methods depend on the store settings and are shown at checkout: debit / credit card (Visa, Mastercard,
UnionPay), mobile wallet (such as JazzCash or Easypaisa), bank transfer, cash on delivery, and gift card.
Card and wallet payments are made on a secure payment page; the store never sees full card numbers.
Cash on delivery means paying in cash when the parcel arrives; it may be limited to orders up to a maximum amount.

## Payment Failed or Not Completed

If an online payment fails or is not finished, the order stays as "Awaiting payment" and the order page shows
a "Pay now" button to try again. If it is not paid in time, the order is cancelled automatically and the items
go back on sale. If money was taken from the account but the order still shows "Awaiting payment", talk to the support team.

## Coupons and Gift Cards

Enter a coupon code at checkout; the discount is shown before placing the order. Each coupon has its own rules
(minimum order, dates, products) and the checkout explains why a code does not apply.
Gift card codes are also entered at checkout and can pay for part or all of an order.

## Order Status Meanings

Awaiting payment: the online payment is not completed yet.
Paid: the payment was received.
Confirmed: a cash on delivery order is confirmed.
Processing: the seller is preparing the items.
Packed: the parcel is packed and waiting for the courier.
Shipped: the parcel is with the courier; a tracking number is emailed.
Out for delivery: the courier is delivering it today.
Delivered: the parcel was delivered.
Cancelled: the order was cancelled.
Return requested: a return is in progress.
Returned: the returned items reached the seller.
Refunded: the money was sent back.
With several sellers, each seller's part of the order has its own status.

## Tracking an Order

Signed-in customers: Account, then My orders, then open the order to see each seller's status, courier and tracking number.
Guests: open the Track order page and enter the order number and the email used at checkout.
When a parcel ships, an email with the tracking number is sent.

## Cancelling an Order

An order can be cancelled from its order page ("Cancel order") while every seller's part is still Awaiting payment,
Paid, Confirmed, Processing or Packed. Once any part has shipped it can no longer be cancelled; after delivery a return
can be requested instead. When a paid order is cancelled, the refund is sent to the original payment method and an email confirms it.

## Returns

After delivery, open the order and choose "Return items" within the return window. Pick the items and quantities and a reason:
arrived damaged, wrong item sent, not as described, missing parts or accessories, no longer needed, or other.
Each request gets a return number like RMA-…. The seller approves or rejects it; once approved, send the items back as
instructed. Track returns under Account, then Returns. A pending return can be cancelled from its page.

## Refunds

Refunds are sent to the original payment method once the seller has received the returned items, or right away when
a paid order is cancelled. An email confirms every refund. How long the money takes to appear depends on the bank or wallet.
If a refund has not arrived, talk to the support team with the order or return number.

## Invoices

The invoice of an order can be downloaded as a PDF from the order page.

## Buy Again

From a past order, "Buy again" puts the same items back in the cart (items that are no longer sold or out of stock
are skipped and listed).

## Loyalty Points

Points are earned on orders and with bonuses (signing up, reviewing products, referring friends) and can pay for part of
an order at checkout. See them under Account, then Loyalty, together with the referral link. Points expire after a while.

## Referring Friends

Every account has a referral code (Account, then Loyalty). A friend enters it when registering; when they order,
both can receive bonus points as set by the store.

## Reviews and Questions

Customers can review products they bought. Anyone can ask a question on a product page; sellers answer, and questions
may be checked before they appear.

## Wishlist, Addresses and Emails

Save products to the wishlist with the heart button. Manage delivery addresses under Account, then Addresses.
Choose which emails to receive (offers, newsletter) under Account, then Preferences; every marketing email also has
an unsubscribe link.

## Seller Policies

Each seller can publish their own policies (returns, warranty, delivery) on their store page. The store's return
window and rules apply to every order; a seller's policy can add details for their products.

## Problems with a Delivered Order

Item damaged, wrong, or parts missing: request a return from the order page within the return window and choose the
matching reason and describe the problem in the comments. The seller reviews it. If the seller rejects a return the
customer disagrees with, or a seller does not respond, talk to the support team.

## Privacy and Your Data

The store only uses personal data to run orders, accounts and (if allowed) emails. To change what you receive use
Account, then Preferences. For a copy of your data or to close your account, talk to the support team.

## Selling on the Marketplace

Businesses can apply to become a seller through the seller center. The store reviews each application; approved sellers
list products (checked before publishing), ship their own orders and are paid out regularly.

## Talking to a Person

Customers can ask for a person at any time ("Talk to a person"). Problems that need the support team: money taken but
the order shows unpaid, a damaged, wrong or missing item, a late refund, an account that cannot be accessed, or a complaint.
Include the order number when there is one.
